Dungeon Jump Adventures

Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version: Dungeon Jump Adventures does not collect any information about you. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no code from other companies. Your progress stays on your device.

Who this covers

This policy covers the iPhone game Dungeon Jump Adventures and this website. The game is made by an independent developer. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean that developer.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, send, sell, or share personal information. I receive no data from the game, and I have no way to identify you.

What is saved on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the dungeon you have reached, your coins, and your scores
  • the characters and trails you own, and the ones you have equipped
  • whether you have seen the how-to-play cards

The game never sends this anywhere. Deleting the game deletes it.

If you back up your device, to iCloud or to a computer, the backup may include this saved data. Backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by me.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases of characters and trails. Apple handles every purchase through the App Store. I never see your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account.

Apple tells the game which items have been bought, and nothing else. The game uses that to unlock them, including after you reinstall the game or tap Restore Purchases.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your score to Game Center so that it can appear on the leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple. Your Game Center nickname and your scores are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

The game works the same without Game Center. You can sign out of it in the Settings app.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting code from other companies.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-up.

Statistics from Apple

Apple gives developers summary statistics about their apps, such as the number of downloads and crash reports. They are anonymous and combined across many people, and they include you only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in the Settings app. They do not identify you.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. I do not use them.

Your choices

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to provide, correct, or delete. To remove the data saved on your device, delete the game.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this policy before that change is released, and I will change the date at the top of this page.
