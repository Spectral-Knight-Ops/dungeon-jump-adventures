Dungeon Jump Adventures

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: Dungeon Jump Adventures has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. I collect nothing about you. The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, and those ads are never personalised. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to your own iCloud account, where I cannot see it.

Who this covers

This policy covers the iPhone game Dungeon Jump Adventures and this website. The game is made by an independent developer. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean that developer.

What I collect

Nothing. The game does not send me any information, and I have no way to identify you. Three companies handle parts of the game on my behalf: Apple (purchases, Game Center, iCloud) and Google (the optional ads). What each of them receives is described below.

What is saved on your device

The game saves your progress so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the dungeon you have reached, your best dungeon, your coins, and your scores
  • the characters and trails you own, and the ones you have equipped
  • whether you have seen the how-to-play cards, and how many free-coin ads you have watched today

iCloud backup

The game keeps a copy of that progress in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. This is what brings your coins, characters and progress back if you delete the game, reinstall it, or move to a new iPhone. The copy lives in your account and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. I cannot see it.

It only works when you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud enabled for the game. You can turn it off, or delete the game’s iCloud data, in the Settings app under your name, then iCloud. With it off, your progress stays on your device only.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases: packs of coins, and a one-time unlock of every character and trail. Apple handles every purchase through the App Store. I never see your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account.

Apple tells the game what you have bought, and nothing else. The one-time unlock can be restored from Apple at any time with Restore Purchases. Coins, and anything bought with coins, come back through your iCloud backup.

Ads

The game shows an ad only when you ask for one, in two places: to double the coins from a cleared dungeon, and to earn a few coins from the Merchant. There are no other ads. If you never tap those buttons, you never see an ad.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. I ask Google for non-personalised ads only: ads chosen without using your interests or your activity in other apps and websites. The game does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking.

If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent screen once before any ad can be requested, and respects your answer. You can change it at any time from Privacy Options in the Merchant’s Get Coins panel. Players elsewhere do not see this screen.

To serve an ad, measure it, and prevent fraud, Google receives some technical information from your iPhone when an ad is requested, such as your IP address, the device model and iOS version, the game’s identifier, and whether the ad was shown or tapped. If you tap an ad and install the app it advertises, Apple’s privacy-preserving attribution (SKAdNetwork) may tell the advertiser that an install came from an ad, without identifying you. Google’s handling of this information is described in Google’s privacy policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best dungeon and your scores to Game Center so that they can appear on the leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple. Your Game Center nickname and your scores are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

The game works the same without Game Center. You can sign out of it in the Settings app.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no ad that you did not ask for.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and never asks permission to.
  • It contains no analytics or crash-reporting code from other companies. The only code from another company is Google’s ad library.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-up.

Statistics from Apple

Apple gives developers summary statistics about their apps, such as the number of downloads and crash reports. They are anonymous and combined across many people, and they include you only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in the Settings app. They do not identify you.

Children

I collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are non-personalised for every player, and an ad is only ever shown when a button is tapped to ask for one.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. I do not use them.

Your choices

  • Ads: simply do not tap the ad buttons. The game never shows one on its own.
  • iCloud backup: turn it off for the game in the Settings app, under your name, then iCloud.
  • Game Center: sign out in the Settings app.
  • Data on your device: delete the game.

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to provide, correct, or delete. For information held by Apple or Google, use their own privacy tools, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this policy before that change is released, and I will change the date at the top of this page.
