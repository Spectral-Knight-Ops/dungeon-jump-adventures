Common questions

How do I play?

Your hero bounces on its own. Tap anywhere on the screen to place a platform there, and your hero will bounce off it. Each platform works once. Climb to the finish line at the top of the dungeon, and stay out of the lava at the bottom. The How To Play button on the title screen shows the rules at any time.

I bought a character or a trail and it is missing.

Open the Merchant from the title screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so they come back on any iPhone signed in to the same account.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store purchases and refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the game removes it. Your purchases are not lost. Use Restore Purchases to get them back.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. You only need a connection to make or restore a purchase, or to use the Game Center leaderboard.

Are there ads?

No.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later.

What information does the game collect?

None. The privacy policy explains this in full.
