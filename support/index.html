Common questions

How do I play?

Your hero bounces on its own. Tap anywhere on the screen to place a platform there, and your hero will bounce off it. Each platform works once. Climb to the finish line at the top of the dungeon, and stay out of the lava at the bottom. The How To Play button on the title screen shows the rules at any time.

How do I get characters and trails?

With coins, at the Merchant on the title screen. Coins come from clearing dungeons, from the optional ads, and from coin packs. There is also a one-time purchase that unlocks every character and trail.

I bought something and it is missing.

If you bought the unlock-everything purchase, open the Merchant, tap Get Coins, then Restore Purchases. It belongs to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any iPhone signed in to the same account. Coins, and anything bought with coins, come back through iCloud: make sure you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud enabled for the game, and give it a minute. If they still do not appear, send an email.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store purchases and refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Not if you use iCloud. The game keeps a backup of your progress, coins, and unlocks in your own iCloud account, and brings it back when you reinstall or move to a new iPhone. If iCloud is off for the game in the Settings app, your progress is saved only on your device and deleting the game removes it.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. You only need a connection to make or restore a purchase, to watch an ad for coins, to back up to iCloud, or to use the Game Center leaderboards.

Are there ads?

Only if you ask for one. After clearing a dungeon you can watch an ad to double its coins, and the Merchant offers a few free coins for watching one. The game never shows an ad on its own, and ads are never personalised.

Can I go back to an easier dungeon?

Yes. On the title screen, the arrows either side of the dungeon number step between dungeon 1 and the best dungeon you have reached. Your best is never lost.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later. The game was built and tested on iOS 26. If something looks wrong on an older version, please send an email and say which iPhone and iOS version you have.

What information does the game collect?

Nothing that identifies you, and nothing at all is sent to me. The privacy policy explains what Apple and Google handle on the game’s behalf.
